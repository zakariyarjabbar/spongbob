Generated environmental artwork: `/assets/ocean.jpg` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. It is an original cartoon underwater background. No third-party license status or commercial clearance is asserted here.

Generation prompt:

> Use case illustration-story. Asset type: wide landscape underwater cartoon environment background for a playful merchandise website promotional banner. Original 2D hand painted TV animation style underwater ocean panorama, bright turquoise and cyan water, giant simple faint five-petal underwater flower outlines floating in the water, sandy seabed along the bottom 20 percent with little rounded pastel pink coral, curved seaweed, cobalt blue tube coral, small bubbles. Yellow and orange sea anemones. Warm nostalgic whimsical cartoon watercolor, slightly textured paper, black hand inked outlines for foreground forms. Wide horizontal landscape, no characters, no people, no recognizable franchise buildings, no text, no logos, no letters, no UI. Center-left open turquoise clear negative space for web heading. Densest coral on lower right and lower left. Excellent polished illustration, not 3D.
